An Asian hair specialty

Cutting and coloring for Asian hair takes a different read on density, weight, and how a strand curls. Tiffany Hair's digital perms and K-Beauty inspired finishes are built around that, using modern rod and heat techniques for texture that moves and holds without weighing hair down.

  • Digital perms for soft, lasting movement
  • K-Beauty inspired color and finishing
  • Cuts tailored to Asian hair density and texture
